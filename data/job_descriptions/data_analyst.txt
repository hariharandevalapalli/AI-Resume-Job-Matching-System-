Data Analyst

About us
We are a fast-growing retail company that relies on data to make decisions.

The role
You will turn raw data into insights for our sales and operations teams, building dashboards and answering business questions.

Requirements
- 2+ years of experience in data analysis
- Advanced SQL skills
- Experience building dashboards in Tableau or Power BI
- Strong Excel skills and solid understanding of statistics
- Excellent communication skills and ability to present findings to stakeholders
- Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field, business or economics

Nice to have
- Python with Pandas for data analysis
- Experience with Snowflake or another cloud data warehouse
- A/B testing experience is a plus
